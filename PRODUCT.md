# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Individual real estate agents managing their own leads and listings.

## Product Purpose
LeadPilot is a modern CRM and dashboard designed to help real estate agents easily track site visits, manage leads, and add property listings. Success means saving the agent time and increasing deal closure rates through efficient tracking.

## Positioning
Automated CRM workflows (follow-ups, visit scheduling) built into a premium, ultra-responsive interface. 

## Operating Context
Agents use this tool throughout their day in a fast-paced environment, frequently jumping between property visits, client calls, and desk work. The UI must be highly scannable and instantly responsive.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Managing and adding property listings with image uploads.
- Tracking leads and their match preferences.
- Managing upcoming site visits and daily tasks.
- "Smart Matcher" functionality to connect leads with ideal properties.
- Built on Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS.
- Real-time/optimistic UI updates using SWR.

## Brand Commitments
No formal logo or strict brand guidelines yet, but we are committed to an ultra-premium, dark aesthetic (using Cormorant Garamond for elegant typography and glassmorphic elements for depth).

## Product Principles
1. **Automation-first**: Automated workflows (like follow-ups and scheduling) should drive the experience to minimize manual data entry.
2. **Premium feel**: The tool itself should feel as high-end and luxurious as the properties being sold.
3. **Clarity over clutter**: Crucial information (tasks, visits, leads) must be scannable at a glance without overwhelming the user.
