# 🌟 LeadPilot Client & Real Estate Agent User Guide

Welcome to **LeadPilot**, your AI-powered Real Estate Sales CRM and intelligent qualification assistant.

---

## 🎯 What LeadPilot Does For You

1. **Instant 24/7 AI Qualification**: Responds to inbound leads on WhatsApp within seconds, warms them up, qualifies their budget & locality, and recommends properties from your catalog.
2. **Effortless Property Ingestion**: Add properties in 5 seconds by simply pasting WhatsApp builder broadcasts or broker notes.
3. **Custom AI Sales Pitching**: Train your AI with your agency's exact negotiation guidelines, site visit hours, and target customer pitch.
4. **Agent Takeover & Alerting**: When a lead shows high buying intent (e.g. requests a site visit or wants to negotiate), the AI marks the lead as **Hot / Needs Agent** and schedules a task on your dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start in 3 Easy Steps

### Step 1: Set Your AI Persona & Custom Rules
1. Click the **Gear (⚙️) icon** in the top navigation bar.
2. In the **🤖 AI & Custom Prompts** tab:
   - Enter your **Agent Name** and **Agency Name**.
   - Pick your **AI Tone** (e.g., *Warm & Approachable*, *Luxury Real Estate Consultant*, or *Fast & Crisp*).
   - Select your **Language Style** (e.g., *Hinglish* for natural Indian chats, *English*, or *Auto*).
   - In **Custom Instructions**, type any specific rules you want your AI to follow (or click the quick template buttons below the box):
     - *Example*: *"Always pitch our Indiranagar 3BHK project first."*
     - *Example*: *"Site visits are strictly available on Saturday & Sunday mornings."*
     - *Example*: *"If the buyer is an NRI, highlight 7% rental yield and RERA clearance."*
3. Click **Save AI Instructions**. (Takes effect immediately on all incoming messages!)

---

### Step 2: Add Your Properties (The Ultra-Fast AI Way)
1. Go to the **Properties** tab in the top navigation.
2. Click **+ Add Property**.
3. Choose **✨ AI Fast Import (Paste Text)**.
4. Paste any raw message you received from a builder or WhatsApp group, for example:
   > *"Godrej Palm Retreat 2BHK in Sarjapur Road Bangalore. 1200 sqft. Asking ₹85 Lakh - ₹98 Lakh. Pool, Gym, Kids Play Area. Possession Dec 2026. Unfurnished."*
5. Click **Auto-Fill Form with AI**.
6. The AI instantly extracts the Title, BHK, Locality, Price in Crores/Lakhs, Area, and Description!
7. Click any **1-Click Image Preset** (e.g. *Modern Villa* or *Luxury Apartment*) or add your own image links, then click **Save Property**.

---

### Step 3: Managing Leads & Site Visits
1. On the **Leads** dashboard, leads are color-coded by AI Score (**Hot**, **Warm**, **Cold**).
2. Click on any lead to see their **Full Intelligence Summary** (Budget, Locality, Moving Timeline, and WhatsApp conversation history).
3. Check the **Upcoming Visits** widget for site visits scheduled automatically by the AI.
4. Click **Bulk WhatsApp** to send a personalized announcement or new project launch to multiple selected leads at once!
